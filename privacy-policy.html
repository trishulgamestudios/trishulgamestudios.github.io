5. Advertising and your ad choices

The game is free and paid for by ads. Ads may be personalised (based on your advertising ID and activity across apps) or contextual (not based on your profile).

  • Consent prompt: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where else the law requires, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Partner opt-outs: you can also opt out directly through each ad partner's policy linked in section 4.

Opting out does not remove ads; you will still see ads, just less tailored to you.

6. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK, or the higher age your country sets). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If we learn that a user is under that age, we will stop personalised ads for that user and delete their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us at the address in section 9.

7. Retention, security and transfers

  • Retention: we keep your game progress and player ID until you delete them with the Delete User button in the game's Settings, or ask us by email to delete them. We keep support emails only as long as needed to answer you. Partners keep data under their own retention rules.
  • Security: data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Unity's cloud services, which use industry-standard safeguards. No system is 100% secure.
  • International transfers: we and our partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States and India. For transfers from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict processing, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier processing);
  • data portability;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising (California, Virginia, Colorado and other US states);
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights;
  • complain to your local data protection authority (EEA/UK) or, in India, the Data Protection Board under the DPDP Act 2023.

How to ask: to delete your data, tap Delete User in the game's Settings; this permanently removes your player account and saved progress from our servers. If you no longer have the game, follow the steps on our Delete your account page. For any other request, email us at the address in section 9. We answer within 30 days, or sooner if your local law requires. We may need to confirm the request comes from you.

Uninstalling the game stops further collection but does not delete data already on our servers; use Delete User in Settings before uninstalling. Data held by ad partners is deleted through their own policies linked in section 4.

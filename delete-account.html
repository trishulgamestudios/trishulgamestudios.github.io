Delete your account and data

Home Decor · Trishul Game Studios

You can delete your Home Decor account and all the game data we hold about you at any time, free of charge. Choose either option below.

Option 1: Delete in the game (instant)

  1. Open Home Decor and make sure you are connected to the internet.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete User.
  4. When asked "Are you sure you want to delete your account?", tap Yes.

Your account and saved progress are removed from our servers straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Use this if you no longer have the game installed, or the in-game option doesn't work for you.

  1. Email trishulgamestudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account - Home Decor".
  2. Include your Player ID, so we can find your account (see below).
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Finding your Player ID

  1. Open Home Decor.
  2. On the main menu, tap your profile picture (top left).
  3. Under your username, find Player ID and tap the copy icon next to it.
  4. Paste it into your email.

No longer have the game installed? Send the Google Play Games username you signed in with instead, with a screenshot of your Google Play Games profile if you can, and roughly when you started playing.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account and player ID
  • Your saved progress on our servers: levels, rooms, coins, lives and settings

What may be kept

  • Your deletion email: we keep it only as long as needed to confirm the deletion, then delete it.
  • Ad partners: Unity Ads and ironSource hold advertising data under their own policies. You can ask them to delete it through the Unity Game Player Privacy Policy, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.

Playing as a Guest?

Guest progress is stored only on your phone, not on our servers. To delete it, open your phone's Settings > Apps > Home Decor > Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the game. Android may also keep a backup of app data in your Google account; you can remove it in your phone's Google backup settings.
